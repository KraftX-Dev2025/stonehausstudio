Stone Haus.

Stone Haus is a creative studio and cultural space in Koregaon Park, Pune — built for photographers, brands, directors and makers who need a room that gets out of the way and lets the work breathe.

Approach

We believe a space that calms the room makes better work. By keeping our walls neutral, our daylight soft, and our footprint flexible, we let the brand, the photographer, and the moment do the talking.

Stone Haus opened with one idea — do things differently. We listen before we build. We host before we charge. We're practical, not bland.

Co-creation

The best shoots happen when the studio is invisible. We sit with you before your shoot, learn the brief, set up the rig, and step back. Productions move faster when nothing in the room fights the work.

Press

We've been covered by Architectural Digest India, Condé Nast Traveller and Verve as one of Pune's new creative addresses. We're quietly proud of it, but the calmest studio in the city doesn't need a banner.

Futures

We believe everyone is creative. Outside of bookings, we mentor photographers, host monthly community evenings, and fund a small grant for first-time exhibition makers from Pune.

If your project sits between commercial and curious — we're probably the right room.
